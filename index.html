What's inside

Home

A daily briefing that surfaces the single most useful thing to do — and says so plainly when nothing needs your attention.

Activity

Every transaction, import, and reconciliation lives in one searchable log — filterable by account, category, or date range.

Plan

Position

Net worth, always current

Accounts, investments, and loans roll up into one number — tracked automatically as you record activity, not once a year.

Locked to your device

Private by design.
Always offline.

Money Manager was built on one rule: your financial data never has to leave the device it's on. Even syncing to your phone happens over your own Wi-Fi — never through a server.

Encrypted local database

Your ledger is protected with SQLCipher and a key tied to your own Windows account — not a password on someone else's server.

No account, no ads, no trackers

There's nothing to sign up for and nothing watching. Money Manager works fully offline, out of the box.

Phone sync, without the cloud

Pair your Android app over local Wi-Fi with a one-time QR code. Each device keeps its own encrypted database.
